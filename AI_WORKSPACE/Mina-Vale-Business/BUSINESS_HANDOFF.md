# Mina Vale — Business Handoff

## Objective

Build a clearly disclosed fictional adult virtual influencer named Mina Vale. Use public-safe lifestyle content on Instagram and TikTok to attract English-speaking adult men in the United States and Canada, then convert qualified followers into paying Fanvue subscribers for premium photo, video and chat experiences.

## Audience hypothesis

- Adults only; primary working hypothesis: men approximately 30–80 in the United States and Canada.
- English-language content.
- Middle to upper-middle disposable income.
- Interested in attractive adult creators, Asian-inspired beauty, curvy athletic proportions, personalized attention, chat and paid photo/video packs.
- This is a hypothesis requiring validation through platform analytics, not a confirmed market fact.

## Brand and character

- Name: Mina Vale.
- Clearly fictional AI-generated adult creator, visual age approximately 30.
- Identity anchor: softly heart-shaped oval face, dark-brown almond eyes, long near-black hair, one narrow burgundy-copper face-framing streak, warm light-medium skin, copper moon necklace, curvy athletic hourglass proportions.
- Voice: confident, playful, warm, aspirational and conversational; never deceptive about being AI-generated.
- Current Eromify handle: `@mina`.

## Funnel

1. Instagram and TikTok: public-safe lifestyle, fitness, fashion, travel, gaming, swimwear and suggestive-but-covered teasers.
2. Profile and link destination: transparent AI disclosure, age gate and clear value proposition.
3. Fanvue: subscription content, premium sets, paid packs and personalized chat within platform rules.
4. Retention: consistent posting, recurring themes, audience polls, serialized sets and responsive messages.

## Current production system

- Eromify is the current image-generation platform.
- A master identity image and three consistency views are stored in `assets/approved/`.
- Eight public-safe master scenes have been generated and visually reviewed.
- Prompt libraries define the identity lock, public scenes, premium sensual scenes and cost-control rules.
- Generation rule: exactly one image per paid action, wait for the new asset and updated balance, and never retry a silent request automatically.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No use of a real person’s identity or likeness without documented permission.
- No minors or ambiguous age presentation.
- No deceptive claim that Mina is a real human.
- Public social-media content must follow each platform’s current rules.
- Premium or explicit source material must never be committed to this public repository.
- Never publish credentials, account details, payment information, customer data or private conversations.

## Honest status

The visual foundation is usable, but the business is not yet proven or ready to promise predictable income. Consistency, account positioning, publishing cadence, conversion tracking and Fanvue monetization still require implementation and real-market testing.
