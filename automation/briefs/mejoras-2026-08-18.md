# Briefs de mejora · 2026-08-18

## `/general-construction/green-bay-wi/`

Ranked by expected impact. Recommendations 1–3 are the highest-leverage on-page fixes; 4–6 are supporting; 7 is a reality check.

### 1. Rewrite the title tag — the biggest single lever
Every query you want to win says "general **contractor**," but the title leads with "general **construction**" and then repeats the brand and category, wasting SERP pixels.

Current:
> General Construction in Green Bay, WI | Geo Carpentry LLC General Contractor

Replace with:
> General Contractor in Green Bay, WI | Geo Carpentry LLC

55 characters, exact-match on the primary query, brand at the end where SERPs expect it, no duplication.

### 2. Rewrite the H1 so it isn't identical to the title
The H1 is copy-pasted from the title. That reads as templated to the crawler and gives you nothing new to rank on.

Current:
> General Construction in Green Bay, WI | Geo Carpentry LLC General Contractor

Replace with:
> Green Bay General Contractor for Custom Homes, Additions & Structural Framing

Keeps the exact query, differs from the title, describes what you actually build.

### 3. Rewrite the opening paragraph
It currently opens with "Yes —" (reads like an orphaned FAQ answer) and again leads with "general construction" instead of the phrase you want.

Current:
> Yes — Geo Carpentry LLC handles general construction in Green Bay, from new residential builds to large-scale structural projects. As a licensed general contractor headquartered at 735 E Walnut St Suite 3, we've managed major construction projects throughout Brown County for 12+ years.

Replace with:
> Geo Carpentry LLC is a licensed general contractor in Green Bay, WI, handling custom home builds, additions, and structural framing across Brown County. Headquartered at 735 E Walnut St Suite 3, we're your single point of contact from design through final inspection.

Same facts, no invented additions, but leads with the exact query and reads like a human wrote it.

### 4. Add a meta description (currently missing)
No description means Google fabricates a snippet, which suppresses CTR. Low-effort win:
> Licensed general contractor in Green Bay, WI. Custom home builds, additions, and structural framing across Brown County. Free estimate within 24 hours — call (920) 367-1272.

### 5. Add GeneralContractor / LocalBusiness JSON-LD
No schema is visible. Add JSON-LD with `@type: GeneralContractor`, NAP identical to the page, `areaServed: "Green Bay, WI"`, and `sameAs` pointing to the Google Business Profile, Facebook, and Instagram already linked in the footer. Do **not** add `aggregateRating` unless you paste the real live count from GBP — invented numbers get the rich snippet stripped and can trigger a manual action.

### 6. Fix internal anchor text pointing at this URL
Every other page on the site that links to `/general-construction/green-bay-wi/` is currently teaching Google to associate it with the wrong phrase. From the homepage, the four sibling city pages (Appleton, Oshkosh, De Pere, Howard), and any related service pages, change the anchor text to "Green Bay general contractor" (with slight variations, not identical every time).

I would **not** 301 the URL slug yet — that's disruptive and the current slug still contains "green-bay-wi." If items 1–5 plus better anchor text don't move you inside position 10 after 60–90 days, revisit changing the slug to `/general-contractor/green-bay-wi/` with a permanent redirect.

### 7. Honest ceiling — on-page alone will not get you top 3
Position 14–16 in a market like Green Bay typically means Google respects the page's relevance but sees stronger authority elsewhere (older contractor domains, Angi/HomeAdvisor category pages, competitors with dozens of Google reviews). Items 1–6 should realistically move you to roughly positions 8–12. Breaking into the local pack or top 5 organic requires:
- Growing Google Business Profile review count, with the phrase "general contractor" appearing in review text
- Consistent NAP citations (BBB, Wisconsin chamber, HomeAdvisor claim, Yelp)
- One or two topical backlinks from Wisconsin home-improvement or construction sites

Say that plainly to whoever controls the budget — the copy fixes are necessary but not sufficient.

# /services/renovation/

**Query context.** "geo home renovation" is a brand-adjacent query — someone typing "geo" + "home renovation" is almost certainly hunting for Geo Carpentry's renovation service. Position 14.7 with 0 clicks means (a) Google isn't confident this page is the right "Geo" match, and (b) the snippet isn't clickable at that position. Fixes below target both.

## Recommendations, highest impact first

### 1. Add a meta description — the page has none
Zero clicks at position ~15 is almost entirely a snippet problem. Google is auto-generating one from body copy, which starts with a fragment ("Basements. Room additions.") that reads like a poem, not a service description.
Add:
```html
<meta name="description" content="Geo Carpentry — licensed home renovation contractor in Green Bay, WI. Basement finishing, room additions, whole-house remodels across Northeast Wisconsin. Free quote in 24 hours.">
```

### 2. Rewrite the title so "Geo" is the first token
Current: `Home Renovation Contractor Green Bay WI | Geo Carpentry`
Replace with: `Geo Carpentry Home Renovation — Green Bay, WI Contractor`
Puts the brand token "Geo" at the front, where Google matches it against the query and where the human eye scans first.

### 3. Move "Geo Carpentry" into the H1
Current: `Home Renovation & Room Additions in Green Bay, WI`
Replace with: `Geo Carpentry Home Renovation & Room Additions — Green Bay, WI`
Reinforces the brand↔service binding on-page.

### 4. Add FAQPage + Service + LocalBusiness JSON-LD
The page already contains eight fully written FAQs and has no structured data at all. Wrapping the existing Q&A in `FAQPage` schema is free rich-result eligibility and usually the single biggest CTR mover on a service page. Ship alongside:
- `Service` with `provider: Geo Carpentry` and `areaServed: Green Bay, WI + Brown County + Northeast Wisconsin`
- `LocalBusiness` (or `GeneralContractor` subtype) using the NAP already on the page — 735 E Walnut St, Green Bay, WI 54301, (920) 367-1272 — so Google can bind the "Geo" brand token to a verified entity.

### 5. Open the body with a sentence that names the brand and service together
Current first line: `Basements. Room additions. Whole-house refreshes. Open-concept reconfigurations.`
Prepend: `Geo Carpentry is a licensed home renovation contractor serving Green Bay, WI and Northeast Wisconsin since 2014.`
Then the existing staccato line follows. This is the sentence Google is most likely to pull into the snippet if #1 slips.

### 6. Fix the hero image alt text — it currently describes a different service
Current: `Custom home build by Geo Carpentry — Wisconsin general contractor`
Replace with: `Home renovation in Green Bay, WI by Geo Carpentry`
Small signal, but right now the largest image on a renovation page is telling Google "new construction."

### 7. Point one internal link with the anchor "Geo Carpentry home renovation"
On the About page, services hub, and Green Bay location page, change one existing link to this page from `Home Renovation` to `Geo Carpentry home renovation`. Consistent internal anchor is one of the cheapest ways to lock a query→page mapping.

## What will not move without more authority
Even after all seven fixes, cracking the top 3 for "geo home renovation" depends on Google trusting Geo Carpentry as the entity behind the token "Geo." That comes from things this page cannot fix on its own:
- Google Business Profile reviews that use the word "renovation" (not just "deck" or "carpentry")
- Inbound links from Wisconsin trade, builder, and Green Bay-area sites using "Geo Carpentry" as anchor
- Consistent NAP citations across BBB, Angi, Houzz, Yelp, Nextdoor

If a differently-named business is out-linking you for the "Geo" token, no amount of on-page work lands #1. Budget 2–3 months of parallel review and citation work.

# /deck-building/green-bay-wi/

Fetched the live page. The single biggest issue is that this page barely gets shown at all (9–33 impressions per query over 90 days) and when it does, it sits at position 17–30. That pattern says Google is not confident this page is a strong match yet, and no click has ever tested it. On-page fixes 1–5 below will move it. Fixes 6–8 will not move it without more reviews and links.

---

**1. Rewrite the title to lead with the noun searchers actually type. (Highest impact.)**

Current title:
> `Deck Building in Green Bay, WI | Geo Carpentry LLC General Contractor`

Problems: leads with the gerund "Deck Building" while 7 of 8 target queries use the noun form ("deck builder", "deck builders", "deck contractor"); burns the tail on "General Contractor" which is not in the target set.

Replacement:
> `Deck Builders in Green Bay, WI — Wood & Composite | Geo Carpentry`

This covers "deck builders green bay wi", "wood deck builder green bay wi", and "composite deck builder green bay wi" in one title under ~60 chars.

**2. Stop using the title tag as the H1. Rewrite the H1 as a real page heading.**

Current H1 is identical to the title, which wastes the H1 and looks templated to Google:
> `Deck Building in Green Bay, WI | Geo Carpentry LLC General Contractor`

Replacement H1:
> `Deck Builder in Green Bay, WI — Wood, Composite & Custom Decks`

Keep the existing H2 "Deck Building Contractor in Green Bay, Wisconsin" — it usefully adds the "contractor" noun for the "deck contractor near me green bay wi" query.

**3. Split wood and composite into their own H2 sections instead of mixing them in one paragraph.**

"wood deck builder green bay wi" (pos 16.9) and "composite deck builder green bay wi" (pos 20.3) are the two most winnable queries — they're already close to page one. Right now the page treats them as options in a single "What We Do" block. Give each its own H2 with a paragraph underneath so the query has a clean landing target:

- `H2: Wood Deck Builder in Green Bay — Pressure-Treated & Cedar`
- `H2: Composite Deck Builder in Green Bay — Trex & TimberTech`

Do not invent facts about brand certifications. Only state that you build with these materials, which the current copy already confirms.

**4. Add Service, LocalBusiness, and FAQPage schema.**

The audit did not find any visible structured data. For a local service page at position 20+, schema is nearly free ranking + CTR insurance.

- `Service` with `serviceType: "Deck construction"`, `areaServed: "Green Bay, WI"`, `provider` pointing to the LocalBusiness node.
- `LocalBusiness` with the NAP already on the page (735 E Walnut St Suite 3, Green Bay, WI 54301; (920) 367-1272), `priceRange` derived from the ranges already published on the page.
- `FAQPage` wrapping the existing 3 Q&As.

Do not invent an `AggregateRating`. If real Google Business Profile reviews are pulled in per recommendation 5, then and only then add `AggregateRating` with the true count and average.

**5. Pull real Google Business Profile reviews onto the page.**

There are zero testimonials on the page today. For local service intent ("deck builders green bay wi", "deck contractor near me green bay wi"), a page with no visible social proof will not clear position 10 no matter how clean the copy is. Embed 3–5 real GBP reviews with reviewer first name, star count, and date. Do not fabricate names, star counts, or review text. If GBP review count is low, that itself is the ranking ceiling — see item 8.

**6. Replace the SVG placeholder images with real project photos and write descriptive alt text.**

Audit found placeholder SVGs / data URIs and no descriptive alt text. Replace with real photos of decks Geo Carpentry has actually built. Alt text pattern:
> `Pressure-treated wood deck built by Geo Carpentry in Green Bay, WI`
> `Composite Trex deck with railing, Green Bay WI`

Only use photos that are actual project work. Do not use stock.

**7. Expand the FAQ from 3 to 6–8 Q&As using the query language.**

Add Q&As that directly mirror the SC queries and long-tail intent. Suggested Qs (write the As honestly, no invented specifics):

Wire them into the FAQPage schema from item 4.

**8. Honest ceiling: without more Google reviews and local links, on-page alone will not get "deck builders green bay wi" onto page one.**

Impressions of 9–33 per query over 90 days mean Google is barely testing this page. Positions 17–22 for the head "deck builder(s)" queries are held by companies with dozens to hundreds of GBP reviews, older domains, and local news / Houzz / BBB citations. Items 1–5 above will realistically move the page into the 8–15 range. To reach 1–5 you need:
